Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain terms, what happens when you visit this portfolio. The short version: almost nothing is collected, and nothing leaves your device. For how the site may be used, see the Terms of Use.

2026

What This Site Stores

This site keeps three small preference values in your browser's localStorage, so that it can remember how you like it to appear:

  • theme ("site-theme") — whether you prefer the light or dark appearance
  • language ("site-lang") — whether you read the English or the Arabic version
  • motion preference ("site-motion-v2") — whether animations are allowed to run

That is the entire list. These values live on your own device, in your own browser. They are never sent to a server, never shared with anyone, and no profile of you is built from them. They remain only until you clear your site data; after that, the site quietly returns to its defaults and asks you nothing.

What This Site Does Not Use

No cookies. No analytics. No tracking pixels. No advertising identifiers. No third-party scripts of any kind.

Fonts, stylesheets, scripts, and images are all served from the site itself, so loading a page does not contact a third party on your behalf. There is no account system and no login, and this site keeps no database of visitors — nothing about you is collected or stored server-side.

The Contact Form

In the default configuration, pressing Send does not transmit your message anywhere. It opens your own email application with the text already filled in, addressed to nedlsalah@gmail.com. Nothing is posted to a web service, and the site keeps no copy of what you wrote.

What you then decide to send from your email client arrives as ordinary correspondence and is handled that way. If a form endpoint is ever configured so that messages are submitted directly, this page will be updated to say so.

External Links

This site links out to other places: portfolio project sites, Behance, Google Photos. The moment you click, you are on their site, and their privacy policies apply. This site does not control those sites, does not embed their content inside its own pages, and passes no information about you to them — you leave by clicking, and their terms are the ones that follow you there.

Your Choices

You can remove the three preference values at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings; nothing else is needed, and the site simply falls back to its defaults. It will ask you to choose again, and that is all.

If you would like to know whether anything about you is held here, email nedlsalah@gmail.com and ask. Given how this site is built, the honest answer is that nothing is collected server-side: only those three preferences exist, they live on your device, and you control them.

Still have questions about privacy, or anything on this page that reads unclear? Write to nedlsalah@gmail.com and you will get a straight answer.

ما يخزنه هذا الموقع

يحتفظ هذا الموقع بثلاث قيم تفضيل صغيرة في localStorage داخل متصفحك، حتى يتذكر الشكل الذي تفضّله:

  • المظهر ("site-theme") — هل تفضّل الوضع الفاتح أم الداكن
  • اللغة ("site-lang") — هل تقرأ النسخة العربية أم النسخة الإنجليزية
  • تفضيل الحركة ("site-motion-v2") — هل يُسمح بتشغيل الحركات والانتقالات

هذا هو القائمة كاملة. تقيم هذه القيم على جهازك أنت، داخل متصفحك أنت، ولا تُرسل أبدًا إلى أي خادم، ولا تُشارك مع أي جهة، ولا يُبنى من خلالها ملف تعريفي عنك. تبقى موجودة فقط حتى تمسح بيانات الموقع؛ وعندئذٍ يعود الموقع بهدوء إلى إعداداته الافتراضية ولا يطلب منك شيئًا.

ما لا يستخدمه هذا الموقع

لا كوكيز، ولا أدوات تحليل، ولا بكسلات تتبّع، ولا معرّفات إعلانية، ولا أي سكربتات من أطراف ثالثة إطلاقًا.

الخطوط وأنماط CSS والصورات البرمجية والصور كلها تُقدَّم من الموقع نفسه، فالصفحة لا تتصل بأي طرف خارجي نيابةً عنك عند تحميلها. لا يوجد نظام حسابات ولا تسجيل دخول، ولا يحتفظ هذا الموقع بقاعدة بيانات للزوار — لا يُجمع عنك شيء ولا يُخزَّن من جهة الخادم.
