04My Process

A Clear Process from Start to Finish

A structured workflow with direct communication and regular reviews to keep project requirements and expectations clear throughout execution.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    I begin by understanding the project's objectives, target audience, and key requirements, then define the scope of work and expected deliverables.

  2. 02

    Planning

    I develop a practical plan outlining priorities, timelines, and the tools needed to carry out the project according to the agreed requirements.

  3. 03

    Execution

    I work on the required deliverables, whether websites, marketing campaigns, designs, or content, while reviewing progress and coordinating with the client throughout the process.

  4. 04

    Launch & Review

    I review the final deliverables and prepare the project for launch or handover. Where relevant performance data is available, I can help identify opportunities for further improvement.
